## Built this session (DECISIONS #52)

* `design/<character>/brief.json` for all five: ranges, required features, contrast minimums, `dark_ok`
  accents, silhouette thresholds, read-as names and confusions. `animatica.design.brief` validates them and rejects
  references without a license.
* `animatica.design`:
  * `measure`: ten proportion ratios, features and materials.
  * `variants`: `Morph` on a `Species`, which keeps the rig; a fixed 6-candidate spread.
  * `rubric`: weighted plus hard checks, bounded morph edits, a `manual` list for base-mesh work, and `ready`
    gated on the species read and an appeal of 8 or more.
  * `image_checks`: neck notch, floating parts, dark blob, expression change.
  * `critic`: a live vision model in three separate unprompted calls, or a recorded critique pinned to the sheet's
    SHA-256.
* `scripts/design_sheet.py`: front, side, 3/4 and back views, the signature pose, silhouette, 96 px thumbnail and
  3 expressions, plus a score panel. `scripts/design_loop.py`: best-of-N rounds with a contact sheet and
  `loop.json` (winner and two runners-up).
* Adapter: `set_render.transparent` gives RGBA frames, so the silhouette is the exact alpha.
* `docs/LESSONS.md`: each human catch mapped to its check and its test.
* A first loop run on Kiki showed why the critic gate matters. The morph-only rubric reached 10.0 on proportions
  and materials, while the design still reads as a weasel. Only the recorded species read blocks it, so nothing
  can be `ready` without a read.
