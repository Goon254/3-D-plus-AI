## Frame notes (Cycles 960×540, demo scene)

* **f60 (s1 wide/track):** robot faces left, flower tiny at far right; the frame's lower 45 % is empty
  ground — consider lowering the camera / tilting so the horizon sits on the upper third and the ground
  carries texture.
* **f200 (s2 medium/dolly-in):** good composition; sun disc directly behind the robot's head reads as a
  halo — nice, but the disc is a flat sticker (no limb darkening, no bloom).
* **f300 (s3 "closeup"):** this is a medium shot, not a close-up (robot is ~60 % of frame height incl.
  treads). The face screen — where the lip sync lives — is only ~90 px wide; the mouth is unreadable.
* **f470 (s4 wide/crane-up):** clear ending tableau; the ground plane's far edge forms a hard line
  against the sky.
* Workbench preview contact sheet: sun disc is semi-transparent, sky flat, ground almost black — the
  `preview` preset undersells the project badly.
