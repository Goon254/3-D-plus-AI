   `addressee_cut` threshold is 0.6 and it is only "minor".
4. Blue-tinted pebbles on white snow read as puddles; one large flat blue ellipse at ~6 s reads as a pond.
5. Good: the night palette reads as night, the fox hops (shadow detaches), the exit leaves frame and the camera holds.
