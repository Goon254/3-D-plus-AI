## Built (DECISIONS #92): the film's deliverables

`final.mp4` (720p, ≤ 25 MB, soft English captions), `poster.jpg`, `captions.srt` / `.vtt`, `edit.mlt`,

Art-director notes on the choices:

* **Captions**: two lines of ≤ 42 characters, balanced; narrator in italics; a speaker off-shot is named. Kids
  read slowly, so the log lists every cue over 17 characters a second; the voices set the timing, so a fast cue
  is a note for the edit, not something the captioner should stretch over the next line.
* **Poster**: my first heuristic took the shot with the most of the cast. On the film's frames that is a wide in
  which every animal is a few pixels tall, which makes a poor poster. It now takes the first daytime medium shot
  of the lead with one or two friends. On the committed 540p film that lands in SC 4: Tembo, big and readable, with
  Bibi Kobe and the termite mound, under a clean sky; the title reads on the darkened band. It's good, not
  iconic: Tembo looks away from Bibi Kobe there. The owner or the laptop may prefer a hand-picked frame
  (`--poster-at`), e.g. the sunset line-up in SC 7.
* **Edit**: one MLT entry per shot, so the cuts are visible; paths point at the uncommitted scene renders (said in
  the README).

End-to-end in the cloud on ten synthetic 3 s scenes: `final.mp4` = h264 1280×720 + AAC + `mov_text` (eng),
23.1 MB; the MLT parses; the log and poster are written. The real run needs the laptop's `output/savannah/`.
