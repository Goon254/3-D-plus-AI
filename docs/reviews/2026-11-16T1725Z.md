## What I saw

**The fox now reads as a fox.** It has an orange coat, a cream snout and bib, two pointed ears with dark tips, a bushy
white-tipped tail, dark feet and front-facing eyes with catch-lights. In the Cycles previews, a viewer would name it
at a glance. That is the biggest single improvement since yesterday. Weak points:
1. **The critter "close-up" is a medium shot.** The whole body and the lantern are in frame, and the face is about
   20 % of the frame height. The mouth sits under the snout and is barely visible, so the whispered line does not
   read as lip sync. The robot close-up has a big screen face and works. The critter needs a tighter head-and-shoulders
   framing on the muzzle, with the mouth visible below the nose.
2. In the fox close-up the head is still more 3/4 than the 22° measured on the robot. The speaker now opens toward
   the lens, but nothing looks back down at the addressee afterwards. A short glance at the lantern after the line
   would sell the eyeline and add an acting beat.
3. Snow rocks: no puddles any more ✔. But the many small dark stones scattered over the snow read as specks or ash
   in the wides. Use fewer and larger ones on snow.
4. The coat is flat and saturated, with no fur break-up (a noise/sheen or rim to soften the silhouette). It is fine
   for the style, but it reads as plastic in Cycles.

**The HD robot film is good.** It has a warm dusk palette and the sun disc sits on the new hazy ridge band, so the
horizon no longer ends in a hard straight line. Junk is spread around the heap crest ✔. In the 12.5 s dialogue
close-up, the robot faces the lens with the "O" mouth readable and the whole flower in frame. A viewer would call
this frame genuinely nice. Remaining issues: the camera-side forearm floats stiffly off the body edge, and the
motion is still object-level (no bone rig, no secondary overlap on the body). The draft showcase
(`hello-little-one/`) is now stale next to the HD one.
