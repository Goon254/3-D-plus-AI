## Built (DECISIONS #103): neighbours crowding face shots, measured

The second "known, still open" note from the 11-19 film review (legs crowding close-ups at 2:30 and 3:12) had no
check. `animatica.production.framing.crowding` now draws every on-stage actor on a coarse grid of each face shot's
frame, farthest first (body box from the footprint, head box from the rest head), every 0.25 s. A non-subject
whose head is out of the frame and whose body still owns ≥ 6 % of the frame for at least half the shot is a
`crowding` finding. `crowd_ok` on a shot opts out. It runs in `continuity_check.py --cameras` and in
`savannah_scene.py` on the real cameras.
