## What I saw

**The critter close-up is now a close-up.** It is an eye-level head-and-shoulders single with the ear tips in frame.
The head fills about 55 % of the frame height. The cream snout and the open dark mouth are readable at 960×540, and the
mouth shapes change through "Are you lost too?". This was the top P1 item and it is fixed.

**The acting beats read.** On discovery the fox stops short, leans in and tilts its head (8–9 s), then closes the
distance. After the line, it turns its head down toward the lantern with a blink, then comes back to the lens. These are
the first beats in the project that look like a character thinking rather than an object moving.

Weak points, highest impact first:
1. **The glance looks at nothing.** In the close-up the lantern is below the frame. Only its handle loop pokes up at the
   bottom edge, as a small black ring that reads as a stray object. When the fox looks down, the viewer cannot see what
   it is looking at. Fix: either frame the critter single so the prop's lit body is in the lower corner, or keep the
   prop fully out (no slivers). A cutaway insert of the lantern during the glance would be best. The critic should flag
   a prop that is < 25 % in frame as a `prop_sliver` tangent.
2. **The tail blob at the left edge.** The white-tipped tail tip wags into the close-up as a white disc on the frame
   edge. It works in the medium but distracts in the single. Clip it or swing it behind the body for the close-up.
3. Still object-level motion. There is no bone rig, the legs are dark discs that do not step during hops, and the body
   is a rigid sphere with squash. Animation is better but is not yet "a viewer would call it good".
4. The look is unchanged since the last review. The coat is flat and plastic, small dark stones still speckle the snow in
   the wides, and the lantern throws only a faint warm pool. A brighter, larger warm light on the snow would sell the
   "glowing lantern" premise and the night mood.
5. Audio is unchanged: clean, but generic numpy music and no ambience.
