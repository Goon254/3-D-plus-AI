**Kiki v6 — 6/10.** Better than v5: the lighter, softer eye patches look less like a raccoon mask, and the hunch

  base needs a shorter, deeper chest, a bigger head carried higher, and a nose that turns up a little at the tip.

  shows only in the side view.
* **Googly eyes are still there** (open item from 15:36): in the 3/4 and pose views the eyeball's top breaks the
  head's outline, and the brows sit as separate dark arcs above it. The tucked forepaws are still two soft loaves.

**Zuri (brush mane) — 7/10.** The saw-tooth is gone (`mane_brush` 9 %), the face is gentle, hooves are real hooves,
and the stripes wrap cleanly with no bullseyes. The 96 px thumbnail reads as a zebra. But:
* **The mane stops about halfway up the neck.** In the side and 3/4 views it runs from the withers to mid-neck and
  ends as a square-cut striped slab with even height. A zebra's mane runs to the poll, between the ears. No check
  measures how far the mane reaches along the visible neck: `mane_line` runs from the neck's base to its tip, but
  the head mesh covers the top part of the neck, so the mane is buried there.
* The silhouette still reads as a horse or donkey: big barrel, small head, thin legs. Filly proportions (bigger
  head, shorter barrel) would help most.
* The brows float as detached dashes above the head outline in the face close-ups (seen on Tembo and Neema too).
