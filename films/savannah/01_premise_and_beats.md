# Savannah film — premise and beat sheet (draft 1)

**Working title:** *Tembo and the Singing Spring*
**Audience:** children aged 4–9, watched with their parents. **Length:** about 5 minutes (10 scenes plus
title and end cards).
**Tone:** warm, funny and gently adventurous. The suspense is mild: getting lost at dusk, never predators
hunting or anyone hurt.

## Logline
When the watering hole dries up, a clumsy young elephant sets out to find a legendary spring to prove he is
big enough to help. He learns that the journey only works when every friend, fast or slow, tall or small,
brings what they're good at.

## Cast (4–6 animals plus a narrator)
| character | species | role | want → need | gift | flaw / comedy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tembo** | African elephant calf | hero | to prove he's big → to learn that big means bringing everyone along | strength, a kind heart | a wobbly trunk that won't hold water (sprays everyone) |
| **Zuri** | plains zebra (young) | rival-friend | to be first → to wait for others | speed | a show-off who races ahead |
| **Kiki** | meerkat | lookout | to keep everyone safe → to trust her ears when it counts | sharp ears and eyes | sees "danger" in every rock (false alarms) |
| **Neema** | giraffe (young) | gentle friend | to fit in → to stand tall | sees far | shy about her height; bumps into trees |
| **Bibi Kobe** | leopard tortoise | wise elder | to be useful again → (already knows) | memory: she's walked the old path | very, very slow |
| **Narrator** | — | storyteller | — | — | warm, grandparent-like voice |

## Theme
*Fast or slow, tall or small: it takes all of us.* (Show it; say it at most once, at the end.)

## Beat sheet (three acts)
**ACT 1: The dry morning (≈ 1:20)**
1. *Opening image (dawn).* Golden light over the savannah, but the watering hole is shrinking. Narrator
   sets the world.
2. *Setup and flaw.* Tembo tries to drink from the puddle; his trunk flops and sprays Kiki. The friends
   laugh. Tembo is embarrassed.
3. *Inciting incident.* Bibi Kobe remembers the **Singing Spring** beyond the **two bowing baobabs**,
   where water never stops.
4. *Decision.* Tembo: "I'll find it!" Zuri laughs: "You can't even hold a drink!" Tembo goes anyway. The
   friends join; Bibi Kobe insists on coming ("You'll need someone who remembers the way"). Zuri groans.

**ACT 2: The long walk (≈ 2:20)**
5. *Fun and games.* Midday journey: Zuri races ahead and back, Kiki raises false alarms, Neema gets her
   head stuck in an acacia, and Tembo walks patiently beside Bibi Kobe.
6. *Pinch: the fork.* Bibi Kobe says follow the termite mounds; Zuri takes "the shortcut" through the tall
   grass and disappears.
7. *Midpoint.* Late afternoon. A faint cry. Neema stretches up and sees black-and-white stripes far off in
   the grass; Kiki hears where.
8. *All is lost (mild).* Sunset. They find Zuri, scared and sorry, but now they are all lost in grass taller
   than Kiki and darkness is coming. Tembo: "I just wanted to help… maybe I'm too small for this."

**ACT 3: The singing spring (≈ 1:20)**
9. *Dark night of the soul to the turn.* Stars come out. Bibi Kobe teaches them to slow down and look and
   listen. Kiki hears trickling water, Neema sees the two baobabs bowing against the stars, and they walk
   together at Bibi Kobe's pace.
10. *Climax and payoff.* The spring is blocked by a fallen log. Tembo pushes alone and fails, then everyone
    pushes together. Water sings out. Tembo's trunk finally holds water, and he shares the first drink with
    Bibi Kobe.
11. *Final image (dawn).* They walk home side by side, Zuri now matching Bibi Kobe's pace. Narrator closes
    on the theme.
