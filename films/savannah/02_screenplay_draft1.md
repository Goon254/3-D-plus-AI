# *Tembo and the Singing Spring* — screenplay, draft 1

Format: scene heading · time · location · target length. ACTION in plain text. NARRATOR and character
lines in caps. *(intent)* marks the emotional intent for animation, camera and music.

---

**TITLE CARD** — "Tembo and the Singing Spring" over a dawn sky. (5 s)

## SC 1 — DAWN — THE WATERING HOLE (25 s)
*(wonder → worry. Music: main theme on kalimba, gentle; birdsong; wind in the grass.)*
Wide: the sun rises behind an acacia. Grass sways. A watering hole, more mud than water.
NARRATOR: Far away, where the grass grows golden and the sky goes on forever, the rains were late. And
the old watering hole was getting smaller… and smaller… and smaller.
A drop falls from a reed into the puddle. Plip.

## SC 2 — MORNING — THE PUDDLE (30 s)
*(comedy, embarrassment. Music: Tembo's theme, bouncy marimba.)*
TEMBO, a young elephant, trots up to the puddle. Behind him: ZURI the zebra, NEEMA the giraffe, KIKI the
meerkat on a mound.
TEMBO: Water! Finally!
He dips his trunk, sucks, lifts — the trunk wobbles, flops, and sprays KIKI head to toe.
KIKI: (dripping) …Thank you. Very refreshing.
ZURI: (laughing) Tembo, your trunk is a garden hose with hiccups!
Tembo's ears droop. NEEMA gives him a small, kind smile.

## SC 3 — MORNING — BIBI KOBE'S STORY (30 s)
*(mystery, hope. Music: Bibi Kobe's theme, low choir hum + mbira.)*
BIBI KOBE, an old leopard tortoise, plods into frame (very slowly). Everyone waits. And waits.
BIBI KOBE: When I was young… there was a spring… past the two baobab trees that bow to each other. Its
water sings. It never… runs… dry.
NEEMA: A singing spring?
BIBI KOBE: Nobody's walked that way in a long time. Not since… my grandmother's grandmother.
TEMBO: (standing tall) Then I'll find it. For everyone!

## SC 4 — MORNING — THE TEAM (30 s)
*(determination, friendly rivalry. Music: adventure theme starts.)*
ZURI: You? You can't even hold a drink!
TEMBO: I can walk. I can look. And I'm going.
NEEMA: I'll come. I can see a long way. (She bumps her head on a branch.) Ow.
KIKI: Someone has to watch out for danger. (Looks around.) Lots of danger.
BIBI KOBE: And you'll need someone who remembers the way.
ZURI: (groans) We'll be there next rainy season!
NARRATOR: And so, five friends set off: one fast, one tall, one small, one slow… and one with a very
wobbly trunk.

## SC 5 — MIDDAY — THE JOURNEY (35 s)
*(fun and games. Music: travelling theme, shakers + hand drums, upbeat.)*
Tracking shot across open grassland. ZURI trots ahead, circles back, trots ahead again.
KIKI pops up on a termite mound in sentinel pose.
KIKI: DANGER! (beat) …Oh. It's a rock.
NEEMA walks under an acacia; her head disappears into the leaves. Muffled: "I'm fine!"
TEMBO walks slowly beside BIBI KOBE, matching her pace.
BIBI KOBE: You walk slowly for a big fellow.
TEMBO: You'd get lonely back here.

## SC 6 — AFTERNOON — THE FORK (30 s)
*(conflict. Music: tension, slower drums.)*
A line of termite mounds leads left. Tall grass waves to the right.
BIBI KOBE: The mounds. Follow the mounds.
ZURI: That's the long way! The grass is faster. Watch!
ZURI gallops into the tall grass. Her stripes vanish.
TEMBO: Zuri, wait!
KIKI: She's gone. Nobody panic. (Panicking.) EVERYBODY PANIC!

## SC 7 — LATE AFTERNOON — THE CALL (25 s)
*(worry → hope. Music: suspense, soft.)*
A faint cry from far away: "Hellooo?"
NEEMA stretches her neck, higher, higher. Above the grass she sees a flicker of black and white.
NEEMA: There! I see her stripes!
KIKI: (ears up) And I hear her. That way!

## SC 8 — SUNSET — LOST TOGETHER (35 s)
*(the low point, tender. Music: Tembo's theme, slow, minor, solo kalimba.)*
They find ZURI, small and shivering in the tall grass.
ZURI: I thought I was fastest. But fast doesn't help if you're going the wrong way.
They look around: grass on every side, the sky turning purple.
TEMBO: (quietly) Now we're all lost. I just wanted to help. Maybe I'm too small for this.
BIBI KOBE nudges him gently with her head.

## SC 9 — NIGHT — THE STARS (30 s)
*(wonder, turning point. Music: choral pad, all the themes softly together.)*
Stars fill the sky. The grass is silver.
BIBI KOBE: When you're slow, you learn to look up. See the three bright stars? The spring is beneath them.
KIKI: (ears twitching) Listen… water. Singing water!
NEEMA: (neck high) And there: two trees, bowing!
ZURI: We'll go… at Bibi Kobe's speed.
They walk together in a line, ZURI beside BIBI KOBE.

## SC 10 — NIGHT — THE SPRING (40 s)
*(climax, triumph. Music: full theme, marimba + choir, triumphant.)*
Two baobabs lean together. Beneath them a trickle is choked by a fallen log.
TEMBO pushes the log alone. He strains and slips. It doesn't move.
TEMBO: I can't… do it… alone.
NEEMA: Then don't.
Everyone pushes: NEEMA from above, ZURI and TEMBO side by side, KIKI digging under, BIBI KOBE pointing:
"There, push there!"
The log rolls. WATER bursts out, bubbling and singing.
TEMBO dips his trunk… lifts it… and this time it holds. He turns and gives the first drink to BIBI KOBE.
Then he sprays everyone. They laugh.

## SC 11 — DAWN — HOME (30 s)
*(warm resolution. Music: main theme reprise, full.)*
The friends walk home across the golden savannah: TEMBO, NEEMA, KIKI on Neema's head, ZURI walking
slowly beside BIBI KOBE.
NARRATOR: And from that day on, the friends knew something special. Fast or slow, tall or small, it takes
all of us. And Tembo learned that being big doesn't mean doing it alone. It means bringing everyone along.

**END CARD** — "Made with Animatica" (5 s)
