# *Tembo and the Singing Spring* — screenplay, draft 2 (production draft)

Revised after the story editor's notes (`03_story_critique.md`). Each scene lists its emotional intent,
music cue, the gaits it needs, and a shot list. Target running time is **≈ 5:10**.

**Cast and voices** (Kokoro stock voices, assigned in the voice step): NARRATOR (warm, grandparent-like),
TEMBO (elephant calf, boy), ZURI (zebra, girl, quick and bright), KIKI (meerkat, fast, high), NEEMA (giraffe,
soft, shy), BIBI KOBE (leopard tortoise, old, slow, low).

---

**TITLE CARD** — *Tembo and the Singing Spring*, over a dawn sky. (5 s)

## SC 1 — DAWN — THE OLD WATERING HOLE (22 s)
*Intent:* wonder turning to worry. *Music:* main theme, solo kalimba, slow. *Ambience:* dawn birds, wind
in the grass. *Gaits:* none (establishing).
1. EWS: the sun lifts behind a flat-topped acacia; grass sways; distant mountains.
2. WS: the watering hole: cracked mud and one shrinking puddle.
3. CU: a drop falls from a reed. *Plip.*

NARRATOR: Far away, where the grass grows golden and the sky goes on forever, the rains were late. And
the old watering hole was getting smaller… and smaller… and smaller.

## SC 2 — MORNING — THE PUDDLE (32 s)
*Intent:* comedy, then embarrassment. *Music:* Tembo's leitmotif, bouncy marimba. *Gaits:* elephant fast
walk, giraffe walk, meerkat scurry.
1. WS: TEMBO **hurries** in, ears flapping, NEEMA ambling behind, **ducking her head to look shorter**.
   KIKI scurries up a termite mound. ZURI is already at the puddle.
2. MS: TEMBO: "Morning, Zuri! Morning, Kiki! Water!"
3. CU: the trunk dips, sucks… wobbles… flops.
4. MS: the spray hits KIKI head to toe.
   KIKI: (dripping, dignified) Thank you, Tembo. Very refreshing.
5. MS: ZURI: (laughing) Your trunk's a hose with hiccups! (To Neema, kindly teasing) And stand up straight,
   Neema, you lovely lamp-post!
   NEEMA ducks lower, embarrassed.
6. CU: Tembo's ears droop.

## SC 3 — MORNING — THE SINGING SPRING (42 s)
*Intent:* mystery, then hope, then resolve. *Music:* Bibi Kobe's leitmotif (low hummed choir + mbira),
rising into the adventure theme. *Gaits:* tortoise plod; everyone idles.
1. WS: BIBI KOBE plods into frame. Everyone waits. And waits. (A wait gag: Kiki checks an imaginary
   watch on her wrist.)
2. MCU: BIBI KOBE: When I was young… there was a spring past the two baobabs that bow to each other. Its
   water sings… and it never runs dry.
3. MS: NEEMA: A *singing* spring?
   BIBI KOBE: Nobody's walked that way since my grandmother's grandmother.
4. CU: TEMBO, standing tall: Then I'll find it. For everyone.
5. MS: ZURI: You can't even hold a drink!
   TEMBO: I can walk. And I'm going.
6. Reaction beats, one per friend:
   NEEMA (quietly): I'll come.
   KIKI: Someone has to watch for danger. (Looks around.) There's *so much* danger.
   BIBI KOBE: And you'll need someone who remembers the way.
   ZURI: (groans) Then we'll get there next rainy season! (Beat.) …Fine. I'm coming. Somebody has to be
   first.
7. WS, the five set off: NARRATOR: So five friends set off together: one fast, one tall, one small, one
   slow… and one with a very wobbly trunk.

## SC 4 — MIDDAY — THE JOURNEY (36 s)
*Intent:* fun and games, then one quiet breath. *Music:* travelling theme (shakers, hand drums, marimba
ostinato). *Gaits:* zebra trot/canter, meerkat walk and sentinel, giraffe walk, elephant walk, tortoise
plod.
1. Tracking WS across open grass: ZURI trots ahead, canters a loop and comes back, trots ahead again.
2. MS: KIKI pops up on a termite mound into sentinel pose.
   KIKI: DANGER! (Beat.) …Oh. It's a rock.
3. MS: NEEMA walks under an acacia; her head vanishes into the leaves. Muffled: "I'm fine!"
4. Quiet two-shot, tracking slowly: TEMBO walks beside BIBI KOBE at her pace. A butterfly lands on her
   shell.
   BIBI KOBE: You walk slowly, for a big fellow.
   TEMBO: You'd get lonely back here.
   She smiles.

## SC 5 — AFTERNOON — THE FORK (26 s)
*Intent:* conflict. *Music:* tension (slower drums, low marimba). *Gaits:* zebra canter, meerkat sentinel.
1. WS: termite mounds lead left; a sea of tall grass waves to the right.
2. MS: BIBI KOBE: Follow the mounds.
   ZURI: That's the slow way. The grass is faster. Watch!
3. WS: ZURI canters into the tall grass. Her stripes flicker… and vanish.
4. MS: TEMBO: Zuri, wait!
   KIKI: She's gone. Nobody panic. (Beat.) EVERYBODY PANIC!

## SC 6 — LATE AFTERNOON — STRIPES IN THE GRASS (36 s)
*Intent:* worry, then a small triumph for Neema, then relief. *Music:* soft suspense into Neema's
leitmotif (flute-like, airy). *Gaits:* giraffe walk; everyone walks into the grass.
1. WS: the friends at the grass edge. Far away, a faint "Hellooo?"
2. MS: TEMBO: Neema, you're the tallest of all of us. Can you see her?
3. Low-angle CU: NEEMA hesitates… then **stands all the way up** for the first time, neck rising and
   rising above the grass. Music swells.
   NEEMA: There! Stripes!
4. MS: KIKI, ears up: And I hear her. That way!
5. WS: they find ZURI, small and scared in the tall grass.
   ZURI: I thought fastest was best. But fast doesn't help if you're going the wrong way. (To Bibi Kobe)
   I'm sorry.

## SC 7 — SUNSET — LOST TOGETHER (26 s)
*Intent:* the low point, tender, then the hero's choice. *Music:* Tembo's leitmotif slow, in a minor key,
solo kalimba, resolving to major on his decision. *Gaits:* idles.
1. WS: grass on every side; the sky turns purple; the first star.
2. CU: TEMBO: Now we're all lost. I just wanted to help… maybe I'm too small for this.
3. MS: BIBI KOBE nudges him gently with her head. He looks at her: "You'd get lonely back here."
4. CU: TEMBO, lifting his head: Wait. We're not lost if we're together. Everybody: what do *you* notice?

## SC 8 — NIGHT — WHAT EVERYONE NOTICES (30 s)
*Intent:* wonder; each gift clicks into place. *Music:* choral pad with all the leitmotifs layered softly,
one per discovery. *Gaits:* giraffe walk; everyone walks in a line at tortoise pace.
1. EWS: stars fill the sky; the grass glows silver.
2. MCU: BIBI KOBE: When you walk slowly, you learn to look up. The spring sleeps beneath the three bright
   stars.
3. CU: KIKI's ears twitch. KIKI: I hear… water? (Doubt.) What if it's just another rock?
   TEMBO: I believe you.
4. MS: NEEMA, standing tall without being asked: Two trees, bowing! Right under the stars!
5. WS: they walk in a line toward the trees; ZURI walks beside BIBI KOBE, matching her steps.
   ZURI: Bibi Kobe's speed. Everybody.

## SC 9 — NIGHT — THE SINGING SPRING (40 s)
*Intent:* climax and triumph. *Music:* the full main theme (marimba, choir, hand drums), building to a
peak on the water. *SFX:* the strain, a creak, a gush of water. *Gaits:* push poses; idles.
1. WS: two baobabs lean together. Beneath them a trickle, choked by a fallen log.
2. MS: TEMBO pushes the log alone. He strains and slips. It doesn't move.
   TEMBO: I can't… do it… alone.
   NEEMA: Then don't.
3. MS: NEEMA hooks a branch from above; ZURI braces beside TEMBO.
4. CU: KIKI digs out the stone wedged under the log.
5. MCU: BIBI KOBE: One… two… PUSH!
6. WS: the log rolls aside. **Water bubbles up, singing.** Everyone cheers.
7. CU: TEMBO dips his trunk… lifts… and this time it **holds**. He turns and offers the first drink to BIBI
   KOBE.
8. MS: then he sprays everyone. They laugh. KIKI (dripping): …Still refreshing.

## SC 10 — DAWN — HOME (22 s)
*Intent:* warm resolution. *Music:* main theme reprise with full ensemble, ending on solo kalimba.
*Gaits:* everyone walks; Kiki rides on Neema's head.
1. EWS: the friends walk home across the golden savannah: TEMBO, NEEMA (standing tall) with KIKI on her
   head, and ZURI walking slowly beside BIBI KOBE.
   NARRATOR: Fast or slow, tall or small… it takes all of us.
2. MS: TEMBO raises his trunk and sprays a small rainbow into the dawn light, on purpose this time.

**END CARD** — "Made with Animatica" (5 s)

---
**Running time:** 5 + 22 + 32 + 42 + 36 + 26 + 36 + 26 + 30 + 40 + 22 + 5 = **322 s (5:22)**. The
breathing room is deliberate: dialogue timing from TTS will tighten it, and the editor trims holds to
land at 5:00–5:20.

## Gait and acting requirements (to the character and locomotion teams)
| character | gaits | acting / idles |
|---|---|---|
| Tembo | walk, fast walk (no trot!) | ear flaps (happy, droop), trunk wobble/flop and spray, trunk curl to drink, push pose, head nod |
| Zuri | walk, trot, canter | ear swivel, tail swish, head toss (laugh), shy/apologetic head lowered |
| Kiki | walk, scurry (half-bound), climb onto mound | sentinel pose, rapid head saccades, dig, "watch" gag, ride on Neema |
| Neema | walk (lateral couplets) | duck/slouch vs. stand tall (a neck chain pose), head in tree, hook branch, blink, chew |
| Bibi Kobe | plod | head extend/retract, nudge, slow blink, smile |
