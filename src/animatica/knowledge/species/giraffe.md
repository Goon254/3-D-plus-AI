# Giraffe (young) — Giraffa camelopardalis

## Giraffe proportions
* **Seven cervical vertebrae**, the same count as most mammals. The neck is long because each vertebra is
  greatly elongated, not because there are more of them [Wikipedia; PMC5037354]. A large bull's neck is
  ~2–2.4 m long.
* Shape evolution is dominated by **neck elongation more than leg elongation** [Royal Society Open Science
  2015], but the legs are still very long. Forelegs are longer than the hind legs, so the back slopes
  down from withers to rump.
* Our young giraffe: 3.2 m to the top of the head, withers at 1.9 m, neck ≈ 1.1 m, foreleg ≈ 1.45 m,
  hind leg ≈ 1.3 m, body length ≈ 1.1 m. Head ≈ 0.35 m, with a long muzzle.
* **Ossicones**: horn-like knobs of ossified cartilage covered in skin. On females and young they are
  thin and topped with hair tufts; males' are thick and bald from sparring [Wikipedia].
* Coat: irregular brown patches separated by pale lines (pattern varies by subspecies). The tail is long
  and thin, ≈ 0.45 × hind-leg length, with a black tuft.

## Giraffe skeleton → rig
Spine (3), **neck (5-bone chain**: seven vertebrae, merged in pairs for animation), head, jaw, ears L/R,
ossicones (rigid), tail (3 + tuft), legs (two bones each plus a hoof). The neck chain carries most of the
acting.

## Giraffe gait table (walk)
Basu et al. 2019 (zoo reticulated giraffes) [JEB, 10.1242/jeb.159277]:
* Walk = **lateral sequence, lateral couplets**: a hind touchdown is followed *quickly* by the same-side
  fore. This makes the "pacing" look: both legs on one side appear to move together.
* Preferred speed **~1.2 m/s**, mean stride length **2.3 m**, stride frequency **0.5 Hz** (adults).
* **The neck's horizontal acceleration is out of phase with the trunk's**: the neck counter-swings, and
  its excursion grows with speed.
* Free-roaming giraffes go straight from this walk to a rotary gallop: **no trot, no true pace**
  [Basu et al., PeerJ 2018].

| gait | speed (young) | stride freq | β | phase LH LF RH RF |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| walk | 1.0 m/s | 0.55 Hz | 0.68 | 0.00 0.12 0.50 0.62 |
| fast walk | 1.6 m/s | 0.7 Hz | 0.60 | 0.00 0.14 0.50 0.64 |

Body motion: the same-side legs swing almost together, so the body rolls side to side noticeably (±4°).
The neck pitches forward when a forefoot lands and back during its swing, about 2 × per stride, ±6–8°.
The head stays steadier than the neck base (a gaze-stabilising counter-rotation).

## Giraffe behaviour beats
* Browses high: neck up, long tongue curling around leaves (cartoon: a purple tongue).
* To drink it splays its forelegs wide and lowers the whole neck. Slow and awkward: a good comic and
  tender beat at the watering hole.
* Ear flicks against flies. Tail swish. Slow, long-lashed blinks. Chewing when idle (ruminant).
* Gentle and curious; looks down at small friends with the whole neck curving.
