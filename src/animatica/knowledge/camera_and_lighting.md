# Camera and lighting recipes

## Shot sizes
- **Wide / establishing**: whole character + environment; character fills 15–60 % of frame height. 24–35 mm.
- **Medium**: character + the thing they relate to; 30–90 %. 35–50 mm.
- **Close-up**: face, or a two-shot of face + object for dialogue to an object; 50–85 mm, shallow depth of field (f/2–2.8).

## Framing
Rule of thirds: put the subject on a third line with **look room** on the side it faces. Two-shots
centre the pair. Keep the horizon off the centre line. Avoid cutting between two shots of the same
size and angle (jump cut): change size or flip the azimuth.

## Moves
dolly-in = growing intimacy/realisation · dolly-out / crane-up = ending, isolation, time passing ·
track = follow a journey · pan = reveal · orbit = wonder. Ease every move in and out.

## Lighting by time of day
- **Sunset**: low (≈10–15°) warm key from behind/side for rim light, cool blue fill from camera side,
  sky gradient orange horizon → pink band → deep blue zenith; dim the key in the last shot.
- **Dawn**: like sunset, softer and pinker, from the other side.
- **Day**: high white key (45–60°), bright sky fill, slightly negative exposure.
- **Night**: weak cool moon key, dark blue world, warm practical light on the prop (emission + point light).

## Exposure checks
Preview mean luminance 0.07–0.82; < 2 % clipped whites; faces should not be in full shadow.
